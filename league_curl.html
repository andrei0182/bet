Match Odds Overview

The most well-known markets for Premier League betting are described in more detail below:

  • 1x2 - For this market, you bet on either the home team to win, a draw, or away win.

  • Over/Under - Sometimes referred to as the total goals market, you decide whether you want to bet on over or under a line of goals, which could be 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, or even more. If you were to place a bet on over 1.5 goals, you would need two or more goals combined from both teams involved in the match.

  • Both Teams To Score (BTTS) - For the BTTS market, you pick either "yes" or "no" as to whether both teams will score in the match. If you pick "no" and the result is 0-0, your bet or selection will be a winner.
